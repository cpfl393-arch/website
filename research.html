Research

Agenda & projects

I am interested in normative and valence issues — cases where there is broad agreement that a behavior is wrong, and where partisanship should therefore matter least.

These cases are revealing precisely because judgment still varies. Understanding when moral evaluation survives partisan pressure, and when it is absorbed by it, tells us something about the limits of electoral accountability as a mechanism. I approach this through survey-embedded experiments and large-scale electoral survey data, with an empirical focus on South Korea and comparative democracies.

Completed

Thesis

M.A. · Seoul National University · 2025

Valence Issues and Partisanship: Focusing on Normative Issues in the 2021 South Korean By-Elections

Analyzes how voters weighed normative issues against partisan attachment in the 2021 Seoul and Busan mayoral by-elections, using survey data on issue salience and vote choice. Available at the SNU Library and RISS.
